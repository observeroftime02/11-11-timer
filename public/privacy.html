Next 11:11 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Zero Data Collection: Next 11:11 is built with an offline-first architecture. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data, accounts, or private wishes.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal identifiers, names, email addresses, phone numbers, location coordinates, or advertising IDs. No registration, login, or user account is required to use Next 11:11.

2. Local Device Storage

All user data—including your selected favorite cities, custom wish journal entries, and notification preferences—is stored exclusively on your local device storage (localStorage or native device preferences). The developer has no access to your journal entries or settings.

3. On-Device Notifications & Audio

Scheduled 11:11 alarms and peaceful harmonic chime sounds are processed and delivered entirely on your device using Android's native AlarmManager and NotificationManager. No external servers or cloud messaging providers are used.

4. Device Permissions

  • Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to alert you when 11:11 arrives in your chosen time zones.
  • Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used solely to ensure precise 11:11 time accuracy.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official Google Play store listing.
